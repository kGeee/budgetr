# Short #4 — "Where does Apple's money actually go?"

| | |
| --- | --- |
| Platform | TikTok / Reels / Shorts / X |
| Length | 38s |
| Goal | Pure reach. The widest possible top of funnel. |
| CTA | Live web demo (not pricing) |
| Footage | `/fundamentals`, any ticker — **no setup, no keys, no account required** |

**Why this one is different.** Every other short sells a personal-finance feature to
someone with money problems. This one is a *toy* — a beautiful visual answer to a
question people are already curious about. It reaches an audience ten times larger, and
it's the only feature in the app that needs **no keys, no bank, and no account**: it
reads SEC filings directly.

So the CTA is the live demo, not the price. Cold audience, cheap next step.

**Format note:** this is the template for "tool" shorts — any feature that works with
zero setup. Same shape works for the options desk on the free CBOE chain.

---

## Script

**0:00–0:04 — HOOK**

> [VISUAL: empty ticker box. Type `AAPL`, hit enter. The Sankey builds on screen.]

Apple made ninety-four billion dollars last quarter. Here's where every dollar went.

**0:04–0:20 — THE FLOW**

> [VISUAL: follow each branch with the cursor as it's named. Let the diagram breathe —
> this is a *watch it* video, not a *listen to it* video.]

Revenue comes in on the left. It splits immediately — cost of revenue out the bottom,
gross profit carrying on.

Then research and development. Sales and admin. What survives all of that is operating
income.

Take out tax, and the thin ribbon at the end is what they actually keep.

**0:20–0:30 — THE PAYOFF**

> [VISUAL: the three margin tiles.]

Gross margin, operating margin, net margin — computed from the filing, not from anyone's
summary.

> [VISUAL: hit the Annual / Quarterly toggle, diagram re-flows.]

Flip to annual. Type any US-listed ticker and it redraws.

**0:30–0:34 — THE HOOK BEHIND THE HOOK**

> [VISUAL: cut wide to the full app — sidebar visible, Overview behind.]

This is a screen inside a personal finance app I built. Same app tracks your spending,
your net worth, and your portfolio.

**0:34–0:38 — CTA**

> [VISUAL: the live web demo in a browser. Text overlay: **"try it — no signup"**]

Runs on your Mac, nothing uploaded. There's a live demo in the browser if you want a
look. Link in bio.

## Production notes

- **The Sankey is the star.** Resist cutting away from it. Slow the build animation if
  you can — the reveal is the retention mechanic.
- **Pick a company people have opinions about.** Apple, Nvidia, Tesla. Comment-bait by
  construction; the comments will argue about margins and that's free distribution.
- Text overlay the three margin numbers. Muted autoplay is the default.
- **Don't say "SEC XBRL data".** Say "straight from their filings."

## Series potential

This is a repeatable format, not a one-off: *"Where does [company]'s money go?"* — one
per week, same 38-second shape, different ticker. The app is incidental every time,
which is exactly why it works. Nvidia and Costco are the obvious next two (wildly
different margin structures, both visually dramatic).
